Website Project Proposal:

The Copenhagen Bakery Guide

1. Website Title and Topic

Title: The Copenhagen Bakery Guide

Topic: A personal guide to my top five favourite bakeries in Copenhagen, Denmark, featuring bakery recommendations, must-try pastries, locations, pictures, and personal experiences.

2. Why I Chose This Topic

I chose this topic because I think it would be a fun and interesting website to create. Living in Copenhagen, my mom and I would try all the bakeries around town. They are all different and there are different things to get at each so you have to know what to get at each bakery cuz they are known for different things/ i only like certain desserts from each. I wanted to create a website where I could share my favourite bakeries, recommend what to order at each one, and help others discover some of the best baked goods Copenhagen has to offer. Since I already have a lot of personal experiences, pictures, and recommendations to share, I think this would be a great topic to work with.

3. Content for the Home Page and Each of the Five Pages

Home Page: Welcome to Copenhagen's Bakeries

  • Introduction to the website.
  • A short description of Copenhagen's bakery culture.
  • Pictures of pastries and bakeries.
  • A preview of my favorite bakeries.
  • Navigation links to all five pages.
  • Links to explore the different bakery recommendations.

Page 1: My Top 5 Copenhagen Bakeries

This page will introduce my five favorite bakeries in Copenhagen using individual bakery cards. Each card will include:

  • Bakery name.
  • A picture of the bakery or its pastries.
  • A short description of the bakery.
  • Its neighborhood or location.
  • A link to learn more about the bakery.

Page 2: Bakery Profiles 1 and 2

This page will feature detailed information about the first two bakeries. Each bakery profile will include:

  • Introduction and description of the bakery.
  • Pictures of the location and food.
  • Address and neighborhood.
  • My favorite things to order.
  • Must-try pastries and drinks.
  • Approximate prices.
  • My personal experience and rating.

Page 3: Bakery Profiles 3, 4, and 5

This page will feature the remaining three bakeries using the same layout as Page 2. Each profile will include:

  • Bakery description.
  • Pictures of the bakery and pastries.
  • Location and address.
  • My personal recommendations for what to order.
  • Approximate prices.
  • My personal experience and rating.

Page 4: Bakery Comparison Table

This page will feature a table comparing all five bakeries to help visitors decide which ones they would like to visit. The table will include:

  • Bakery name.
  • Neighborhood or location.
  • Must-try pastry or baked good.
  • Approximate price range.
  • Coffee availability.
  • My overall rating out of 5.

Page 5: Reviews and Experiences

This page will allow website visitors to share their own experiences and opinions about the bakeries. It will include:

  • A short introduction inviting visitors to share their experiences.
  • A form where visitors can submit reviews.
  • A section displaying visitor recommendations and opinions, if submissions are available.

4. Form Information and Table Data

Form:

I will create a visitor review form where people can share their own bakery experiences and recommendations. The form will collect:

  • Name (optional).
  • Which bakery they visited.
  • Their favourite pastry or baked good.
  • Their rating of the bakery (1–5 stars).
  • What they liked most about the bakery.
  • Their overall experience.
  • Any additional comments or recommendations.

Table:

I will create a comparison table featuring my five favorite Copenhagen bakeries. The table will probably include:

  • Bakery name.
  • Location or neighborhood.
  • Must-try item.
  • Approximate price range.
  • My personal rating out of 5.

5. How I Will Collect Information, Images, and Content

Most of it will be from personal experiences and opinions from living in Copenhagen and visiting different bakeries. I will use my own pictures of the bakeries, pastries, and food I tried. I will also use information from official bakery websites, online menus, and other reliable sources to make sure the locations, opening hours, and descriptions are accurate.
