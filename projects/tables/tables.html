How to do a Gel X manicure step by step

Prep the natural nails by pushing back cuticles and lightly buffing. Next, choose the correct Gel-X tips and apply the dehydrator, primer, and gel adhesive. Attach the tips and cure them under the UV/LED lamp. Then file and shape the nails, apply your chosen gel polish, and cure. Create your desired nail art, seal everything with a top coat, and cure again. Finally, apply cuticle oil and moisturize the hands.
