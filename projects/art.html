My Art Exhibition

I'm going to talk you through my art exhibiton I created while in school in Copenhagen Denmark. Four years ago I moved to denmark from the states with my parents and little sister. I had to start a whole new school & program and it was honestly pretty difficcult for me. So I decided to major in Art and Business.

Art in the IB entails

  1. The requirment to create a exhibiton with a clear theme and purpose
  2. 10-12 works working with 3-5 different art mediums
  3. Analyzing the entire process & explaining what I did and why I did it

I did my art exhibiton on Emotions specifially the cycle of emotions we experience within our lives. I portrayed this through monocromatic coloring and the female body. It was genuinley such an interensting experience trying to express my feelinngs into my work and protray it in a way I felt other could relate to it as well.

One of my favorite quotes is by Edgar Degas,
" Art is not what you see, but what you make others see. "

I love this quote because I feel like art relates to everyone and it itn't about understanding but about how you personally relate to the art work or how it makes you feel. And when presenting my work and my emotioins on display I was able to see how other people related or how they were impacted by my work.
